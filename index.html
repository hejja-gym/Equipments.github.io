Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 24, 2026

1. About HEJJA

HEJJA is a dedicated team passionate about helping people achieve a healthier and stronger lifestyle. Our goal is to provide high quality and affordable gym equipment that makes fitness accessible for everyone, whether at home or in the gym.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services and products, we collect the following information when you use our website:

  • Account Information: Name, Email Address, Password when you sign up
  • Order Details: Products you select such as strength training tools, cardio machines, and fitness accessories
  • Customer Inquiries: Information you provide when asking for guidance to choose the right equipment

3. How We Use Your Information

At HEJJA, we focus on quality, affordability and customer satisfaction. Your information helps us:

  • Create and manage your account for a seamless shopping experience
  • Process orders for our durable and easy-to-use gym equipment
  • Provide personalized guidance to help you choose equipment based on your fitness needs and goals
  • Improve our products and services to better support your fitness journey

4. Data Protection & Security

We are committed to protecting your data. We implement security measures to ensure your personal information is safe and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

5. Your Privacy Rights

As our customer, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information anytime.
